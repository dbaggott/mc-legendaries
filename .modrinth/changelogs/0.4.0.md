The dragon egg is a legendary now — it cannot be put down, it cannot be lost, and it makes whoever
is carrying it harder to kill.

**The egg the dragon leaves is the legendary.** No craft and no extra step: however you knock it off
the exit portal — onto a torch, with a piston, or with TNT — what you pick up is already one,
shimmering as if enchanted. There is one per world for the same
reason there always was — only a world's first dragon leaves an egg, and re-summoning it never
leaves another. An egg you already had from before this release is an ordinary egg and stays one.

**It cannot be placed as a block.** The dragon egg is the first legendary that was ever a block, and
setting it down would be a way to leave it somewhere. Every route to placing one is refused, and it
says so above your hotbar. Right-clicking chests, doors and everything else while holding it works
normally.

**It goes home like the others.** It is refused by every container, and anything that would destroy
it — an explosion, the void, or five minutes on the ground — puts it on the pedestal instead.

**Carrying it gives you five extra hearts.** They arrive within a second of it reaching your
inventory and go the moment it leaves, by any route — dropped, handed over, died with. If you were
above ten hearts when it went, you are back at ten.

They arrive **empty**, the way Health Boost's do — room to heal into, not five hearts of free
healing. So passing the egg around is never a way to top anybody up.

**`/legendaries config` names legendaries as well as abilities.** `config get dragon_egg` reports
`hearts`, and `config set dragon_egg hearts <n>` turns it — reaching an egg already in a pocket
rather than only the next one picked up. Each subject now lists only the knobs it actually has, so
`config get molten_blast` no longer offers settings that were never its.

`item give` and `item delete` take `dragon_egg` like any other legendary.
