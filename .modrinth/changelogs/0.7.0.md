Two more legendaries, and the first two that are weapons you already know how to make: the
**Legendary Sword** and the **Legendary Axe**.

**Ordinary swords and axes are untouched.** Each of these has a recipe of its own rather than
replacing vanilla's, so everything you could make before you can still make.

**One recipe shape, two items** — what the game's three bosses leave behind, around the tool you are
upgrading:

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| Dragon breath | Dragon head | Dragon breath |
| Echo shard | *Netherite sword or axe* | Echo shard |
| Nether star | Sharpness V book | Nether star |

Two nether stars apiece means two withers apiece, and the dragon head means finding an End city.
These cost more than anything else in the mod.

**The book is checked when you take the result**, the way the pickaxe's are — a recipe can ask for
an enchanted book and cannot ask what is written in it, so the wrong enchantment gives you a result
that refuses to come out and says why.

**The Sword** is unbreakable, carries **Sharpness VIII, Fire Aspect III and Looting III**, and gives
**Speed** while it is in your inventory or your hand. It is now the hardest-hitting thing here: the
Netherite Spear carries the vanilla ceiling of Sharpness V, and this goes three past it.

**The Axe** is unbreakable, carries **Sharpness VIII, Silk Touch and Efficiency V**, and gives
**Strength**. Silk Touch on an axe is what takes a bee nest with its bees still in it. Efficiency V
rather than the pickaxe's VI, so the pickaxe stays the better tool for what a pickaxe is for.

**Both keep every rule the others do** — one per world, refused by every container, never set down as
a block, returned to the pedestal rather than lost. The pedestal now holds six; a world that already
has one rebuilds its plinth on the next load and carries whatever was standing there across, at the
new spacing.

**Both have a look of their own**, from the same optional resource pack the pickaxe uses — the art
shipped inert in 0.6.0 and now has items to attach to.

Still one jar for Minecraft 26.1 through the 26.3 line, and still nothing for other players to
install.
