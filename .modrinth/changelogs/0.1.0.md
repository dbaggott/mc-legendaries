Molten Blast now hurts, and the pedestal is a pedestal.

**The Mace's blast catches what is standing in it.** Everything alive inside the crater except you
takes **two and a half hearts**, which armour does not soften, and is thrown clear as hard as **two
sticks of TNT** — hardest at the centre, fading to nothing eight blocks out, and softened by blast
protection the way a real explosion is. You are spared both: the blast is centred on you, so
charging you for it would tax every use.

**The crater's lining is tunable.** How much of the shell stays as the rock it was found as is now
`unmelted`, a percent `/legendaries config` turns like `cooldown` and `radius`, defaulting to 15.
What melts becomes magma, netherrack or coal, drawn evenly.

**The pedestal is a plinth rather than a single block** — a stepped foot, a lodestone column and a
matching cap, with a purple glass case on top holding each legendary at its dropped size and turning
it slowly. The glass is what you right-click to take one back, and the whole case is the target.
Pedestals in existing worlds rebuild themselves on the next load and carry whatever they were
holding across.

**`pedestal` is now a target for `item give` and `item delete`**, wherever a player selector goes.
`give pedestal` stands a legendary in the case and refuses if that slot is taken, rather than
dropping the copy in the grass; `delete pedestal` destroys what is in the slot, and refuses while
the pedestal has not loaded rather than clearing a slot it cannot see. Both commands also refuse to
hand out an item a datapack has stripped the legendary marker from.

**`/legendaries debug` is gone.** It stood candidate plinth shapes in a labelled row so one
screenshot could settle which to keep; the shape is settled.

Still one jar for Minecraft 26.1 through the 26.3 line, still nothing for other players to install.
