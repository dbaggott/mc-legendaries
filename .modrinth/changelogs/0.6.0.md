The **Legendary Pickaxe** stops looking like an ordinary netherite pickaxe: a gem of molten amber
set into the head, bleeding warm into the metal around it.

**It arrives as a resource pack, and you can say no.** The server offers it when you join, the way
any server offers a pack. Decline it — or turn server packs off entirely — and you get the vanilla
pickaxe and nothing else changes. No other player has to install anything, and an unmodded client
handles the offer like any other.

**Ordinary pickaxes keep their look.** The pack does not replace the netherite pickaxe's texture; it
adds a second appearance that only the legendary selects, and every other pickaxe in the world falls
through to the vanilla one. A client without the pack has nothing to fall through *from*, which is
why declining costs nothing but the look.

**A pickaxe crafted before this update keeps the old appearance.** What an item looks like is fixed
when it is made, and the marker is written by the recipe — so a world whose pickaxe already exists
goes on seeing a netherite one. Nothing rewrites an item you are already holding.

**An admin who already serves a pack keeps serving it.** Set `resource-pack` in `server.properties`
and that answer stands; this only fills a silence.

**Fix: moving the Dragon Egg no longer costs you hearts.** Dragging it from slot to slot — or setting
it in an anvil to rename it — read as having lost it, so the five extra hearts came off and your
health was clamped down to match. Putting it back returned the hearts empty, so every rearrangement
quietly cost real health you could not get back. It now counts as carried wherever you are holding
it, including on the cursor mid-drag.

Still one jar for Minecraft 26.1 through the 26.3 line, and still nothing for other players to
install.
