**Fix: a single-player world would not finish loading.**

Since 0.6.0, opening your own world offered you the resource pack the way a server does — and taking
it left the world stuck on "Loading Terrain". Nothing had crashed; the resource reload the pack
triggers was landing in the middle of the join, and the join never completed.

Only a dedicated server offers the pack now. That is the surface Minecraft itself implements — a
single-player world has never pushed a resource pack, and it turns out there is a reason.

**Single-player installs the pack by hand instead.** The zip is on this release next to the jar and
goes in `.minecraft/resourcepacks`. That is better than the offer was even when the offer worked: it
stays enabled between sessions, where the prompt asked again every single time you opened the world
— single-player has no server entry, and that is the only place a "yes, always" answer could be
kept.

Nothing changes on a dedicated server: it offers the pack on join exactly as before, still optional,
still declinable, and an operator's own `resource-pack` line still wins.

No gameplay changes. Every legendary, recipe and rule is exactly as it was in 0.7.0.
