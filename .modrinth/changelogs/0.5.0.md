A fourth legendary: the **Legendary Pickaxe**. Ordinary pickaxes are untouched — this is the first
legendary that shares its item with something you can still make normally.

**Getting it.** One recipe, built around a netherite pickaxe:

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
| Netherite ingot | Deepslate emerald ore | Netherite ingot |
| Efficiency V book | Netherite pickaxe | Efficiency V book |
| Deepslate coal ore | Sculk shrieker | Deepslate coal ore |

Every ore in it, and the shrieker, are Silk Touch drops — which is what the pickaxe then carries, so
the tool that makes one could have mined its own ingredients.

**The books are checked when you take the result.** A recipe can ask for an enchanted book and cannot
ask what is written in it, so two books of the wrong enchantment assemble a pickaxe that refuses to
come out and says why.

**Carrying it.** Unbreakable, **Efficiency VI and Silk Touch** — a level past what an enchanting
table will give you — and **Fire Resistance** for as long as it is in your inventory or your hand.

**Gravel.** It digs gravel as fast as a netherite shovel does, so with Efficiency VI on top it is an
Efficiency VI shovel for that one block. Nothing else a shovel is for changes. Silk Touch means
gravel always comes up as gravel, so this is not the tool to dig flint with.

**It keeps the rules the others do** — refused by every container, never left on display, returned to
the pedestal rather than lost, and one per world.

**The pedestal now holds four.** A world that already has one rebuilds its plinth once on the next
load and carries whatever was standing on it across, at the new spacing.

**A grindstone no longer strips a legendary.** It used to take anything enchanted, so the Netherite
Spear could be ground back to a plain spear and its Lunge III, Sharpness V, Fire Aspect II and
Looting III were gone for good — no enchanting table will give you those again. Grindstones and
anvils now make nothing at all out of a legendary; you can still put one in and take it back out.

Still one jar for Minecraft 26.1 through the 26.3 line, still nothing for other players to install.
