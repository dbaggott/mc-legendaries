First release.

Two legendary items. Each is one per world, cannot be put into any container, and comes back to a
shared pedestal at world spawn rather than ever being lost.

- **The Netherite Spear** — replaces every vanilla spear recipe with one of Trial Chamber and Nether
  endgame loot. Unbreakable, enchanted at the vanilla maximum of Lunge, Sharpness, Fire Aspect and
  Looting, and grants Speed II while carried. No spear drops from any loot table any more, and no
  mob drops the one it is holding.
- **The Mace** — crafted by the ordinary vanilla recipe, and unbreakable. Sneak and right-click for
  **Molten Blast**: everything within four blocks is erased, and the shell left behind turns to
  magma, netherrack, coal, or is left as it was.

Admin commands under `/legendaries` site the pedestal, hand out or take back legendaries, and tune
ability cooldown and radius in game.

Install it on whichever side runs the world — your own game for single-player, or the server. Other
players need nothing: the mod registers no custom item, block or data component, so a vanilla client
sees only vanilla things.
