The Molten Blast's cooldown comes off the mace and onto the ability itself, counted down above your
hotbar — and the blast throws you less far, with a new voice.

**You can see the cooldown without holding the mace.** It used to be the swipe vanilla draws over an
item in your hotbar, which only works while the thing carrying the ability is sitting in one. It is
now a countdown above your hotbar instead: the ability's name and the time left, ticking down each
second and gone the moment the wait is. Past a minute it reads `1m 23s`.

**The wait belongs to the Molten Blast, not to the mace.** Nothing else carries the blast yet, but
when something does it will share the one wait and the one countdown rather than bringing a second
of its own.

**`/legendaries config` names the ability now.** `config get mace` becomes
`config get molten_blast`, and `cooldown`, `radius`, `unmelted` and `knockback` belong to the blast
rather than to whatever is carrying it. **Anything you had tuned in an existing world goes back to
its default** — the settings are stored under the ability's name now, and the old entries go unread.

**The blast throws you less far.** `knockback` defaults to `150` rather than `200` — a stick and a
half of TNT rather than two. `config` still turns it, and `0` still leaves everything standing.

**The blast has a new voice.** The boom opens with the whoosh of a fireball catching and the hiss of
the whole sphere going out at once, both on the same instant as the explosion. The crater then pops
its way quiet over about a second — lava pops scattered across it, spread in pitch and in time —
rather than crackling like a campfire for twice as long.

Still one jar for Minecraft 26.1 through the 26.3 line, still nothing for other players to install.
