**Molten Blast makes a sound.** The blast lands with vanilla's own explosion, pitched below TNT, and
the crater then crackles itself out over the next couple of seconds — four fading beats scattered
across the crater's own width. Both are vanilla sounds sent as ordinary packets, so a player with
nothing installed hears them.

**How hard the blast throws is now a knob.** `knockback` joins `cooldown`, `radius` and `unmelted`
under `/legendaries config`, counted in hundredths of a stick of TNT and defaulting to `200` — the
strength the blast already had, so an untouched world is unchanged. `100` halves the throw; `0`
leaves everything where it was standing.

**`cooldown` now reaches the wait you are already in.** It is measured from your last swing against
whatever the setting says now, so shortening it past the time already elapsed ends that wait there
and then — no swing needed to clear the old one. Lengthening it reaches the same wait, and can put
the mace back on cooldown after the old one had run out.

**The pedestal's glass case only stands while something is in it.** An empty pedestal is a bare
plinth rather than a display that has lost its exhibit. Pedestals in existing worlds rebuild
themselves once on the next load and carry whatever they were holding across.
